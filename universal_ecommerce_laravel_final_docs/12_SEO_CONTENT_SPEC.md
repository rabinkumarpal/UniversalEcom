# SEO and Content Specification

## URL structure
- `/`
- `/categories/{category}`
- `/collections/{collection}`
- `/brands/{brand}`
- `/products/{product-slug}`
- `/search`
- `/price-lists/{slug}`
- `/knowledge/{article-slug}`
- `/faq`
- `/about`
- `/contact`

## SEO requirements
Each indexable page needs:
- unique title
- meta description
- canonical URL
- Open Graph metadata
- structured data where relevant
- clean heading hierarchy
- crawlable internal links

## Structured data
Use appropriate Schema.org types:
- Product
- Offer
- BreadcrumbList
- Organization
- WebSite/SearchAction
- FAQPage where content genuinely qualifies

## Product SEO
Product page should expose:
- brand
- model/SKU where applicable
- availability
- price
- currency
- images
- aggregate rating only when supported by genuine reviews

## Category SEO
Category pages should include:
- useful introduction
- category-specific FAQs
- buying guidance
- filters that do not create uncontrolled indexable URL combinations

## Content hub
Potential content:
- cement selection guides
- plywood grade comparisons
- waterproofing guides
- electrical wire sizing
- bathroom fitting guides
- construction quantity calculators
- price lists
- project checklists

## Local SEO
If the business operates in specific cities/zones:
- create genuine location pages
- include actual service areas
- include local delivery information
- avoid mass-generated doorway pages with thin content

## Sitemap
Generate:
- pages sitemap
- products sitemap
- categories sitemap
- brands sitemap
- articles sitemap

Exclude cart, checkout, account and admin.
